Score keeper for racket sports. On the first start three questions set it up (sport, singles or doubles, points by tap or by keys only). On the start screen pick a setup (sport and rules), tap NEW MATCH and choose who serves first, or start from a score already on the board. In a match the upper half of the screen or the upper key gives the opponent a point, the lower half or the lower key gives you one; UNDO and MENU are in the middle. RallyPal shows CHANGE ENDS, counts down the rest the rules allow and, if turned on in Settings, adds FAULT and ACE buttons under the score in tennis and padel (after a fault: 2ND SERVE and DOUBLE FAULT). The match ends by the rules or from the menu, and a summary follows. History (with records and heart rate zones), stats and settings are on the watch and on the app's settings page in the Zepp app, where a match can also be added by hand.

Payment: free download with 3 free matches (a match counts from 5 minutes). Then a one-time $2.99 unlock through KiezelPay: the watch shows a code and a QR code, the user pays at kzl.io/code and the watch unlocks by itself. Strava and Intervals.icu sync are optional.
